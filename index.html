How to play

Three ways to study

  • Daily Rounds: tap Start rounds every day. It brings back the questions that are due for review, plus up to 10 new ones.
  • Exam Block: a timed practice test that shows no answers until the end, like the real exam. Choose the exam and how many questions, then tap Start block.
  • Focus on specific topics: tick the subjects, organ systems or topics you want (or the questions you have missed), then tap Start focus.

Answering a question

  • Pick an answer, then tap how sure you were: Guess, Unsure or Sure. Be honest, because it decides when the question comes back. A miss or a guess comes back at the end of the session (except in Exam Block), and sure, correct answers earn the most XP.
  • Read the explanation and why the other choices are wrong, then tap Next.
  • To stop, tap Home at the top of the question (or next to Next), or use your phone's Back button. Every answer is saved as you go. Leaving an Exam Block early shows the review of the questions you answered.
  • If a question looks wrong or unclear, tap Report a problem under the explanation, choose what is wrong, and tap Save report. Your reports collect in the Question reports box on this screen. Tap Copy for my Claude there and paste it into the Fix or change a question prompt at claude.ai/code.
  • On a keyboard: 1 to 5 or A to E picks an answer, G, U or S says how sure you were, and Enter goes to the next question.

On this screen

  • Tap any section's title to fold it away or open it again. This device remembers your choice.
  • My exams: choose the test and its date, then tap Add exam. New questions in Daily Rounds then aim at your next exam, reviews are spaced to come back before it, and Exam Block offers "My next exam".
  • My weak spots: the topics you miss, or only get right by guessing. About once a week, tap Copy for my Claude and paste it into your study buddy chat.
  • Signing in: the game asks you to tap Sign in with Google when you first open it on a device. Use the same Google account on every device, and your progress syncs by itself from then on. There is nothing else to tap.
  • Sync and backup: shows which account you are signed in with. Now and then, tap Save a backup file and keep the file somewhere safe; Restore from a backup file adds one back.
It stays closed until you tap How to play at the top of this screen.

Daily Rounds

Questions that are due for review, plus a few new ones, mixed across organ systems. This is the mode to do every day.

Exam Block

Timed block with no feedback until the end, like the real test (about 90 seconds per question). Pick "My next exam" to match its content and block length. Review every answer afterward.

Focus on specific topics

Tick the subjects, organ systems, or single topics you want to work on, then start. Good for a weak spot; mixed practice in Daily Rounds still builds the longest-lasting memory.
