Med School Prep

Study material for USMLE Step 1 and NBME exams. Not patient-care guidance.

Daily Rounds

Questions that are due for review, plus a few new ones, mixed across organ systems. This is the mode to do every day.

Focus

Drill one NBME subject or organ system. Good for a weak area, but mixed practice in Daily Rounds builds longer-lasting memory.

Exam Block

Timed block with no feedback until the end, like the real test (about 90 seconds per question). Review every answer afterward.

Mastery by system

A question counts as mastered when you have answered it correctly at spaced intervals reaching 3 weeks.
