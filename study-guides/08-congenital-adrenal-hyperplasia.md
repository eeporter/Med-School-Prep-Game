# Congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH)

## In one line
A blocked adrenal enzyme means no cortisol, so ACTH rises and pushes precursors into the pathways that are still open. Then ask two questions. Is mineralocorticoid activity high or low (blood pressure, potassium)? Are androgens high or low (genitals)?

## Core mechanism
1. All forms are autosomal recessive and affect both sexes equally. [StatPearls: CAH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/), [MSD Manual: Overview of CAH](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/overview-of-congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia)
2. The enzyme block lowers cortisol. Low cortisol removes feedback, so ACTH rises. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/)
3. High ACTH makes the adrenal glands grow (hyperplasia) and drives precursors into the open pathways, toward androgens or toward mineralocorticoid precursors, depending on the block. [StatPearls: CAH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/), [MSD Manual: Overview of CAH](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/overview-of-congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia) Your notes add that most forms cause **skin darkening**, because melanocyte-stimulating hormone (MSH) is made and released along with ACTH (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, p. 55).
4. **Mineralocorticoid side:** if aldosterone cannot be made, salt wasting (low sodium, high potassium, high renin). If a mineralocorticoid precursor such as 11-deoxycorticosterone (DOC) piles up, hypertension with low potassium and suppressed renin. [StatPearls: CAH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/); salt wasting in 21-hydroxylase deficiency: [MSD Manual: 21-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-21-hydroxylase-deficiency); DOC excess in 11β-hydroxylase deficiency: [MSD Manual: 11β-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-11beta-hydroxylase-deficiency)
5. **Androgen side:** if the block is below the androgen branch point, extra androgens (virilized girls, early puberty). If the block is above it, too few androgens (undervirilized boys). [StatPearls: CAH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/); MSD: in 21- and 11β-hydroxylase deficiency precursors "are shunted into adrenal androgen synthesis", while in the rarer forms "virilization of males is inadequate". [MSD Manual: Overview of CAH](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/overview-of-congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia)

## The three high-yield deficiencies
| Enzyme | Share of CAH | Blood pressure, K⁺, renin | Androgens | Key lab |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 21-hydroxylase (CYP21A2) | About 95% | Classic salt-wasting: low BP, high K⁺, high renin (about 75% of classic cases); 25% simple virilizing | High: virilized 46,XX girls | 17-hydroxyprogesterone (17-OHP) very high |
| 11β-hydroxylase (CYP11B1) | Up to about 8% of CAH (MSD); second most common (your notes, p. 56) | Hypertension, low K⁺, suppressed renin (DOC excess) | High: early virilization in both sexes | 11-deoxycortisol and DOC high |
| 17α-hydroxylase (CYP17A1) | Under 1% of CAH (your notes, p. 57) | Hypertension, low K⁺, suppressed renin | Low: 46,XY undervirilized or female-appearing; no puberty | DOC and corticosterone high |

[StatPearls: CAH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/), [MSD Manual: 21-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-21-hydroxylase-deficiency), [MSD Manual: 11β-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-11beta-hydroxylase-deficiency)

Memory hook from your notes (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, p. 55): an enzyme name that **starts** with 1 (11β, 17α) gives hypertension; one that **ends** in 1 (2**1**, 1**1**) gives virilization of girls. 11β-hydroxylase is in both lists, so it has both hypertension and virilization.

**Other source (how common):** your notes say 21-hydroxylase deficiency is about **90%** of CAH and 17α-hydroxylase deficiency **under 1%** (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, pp. 55, 57); StatPearls gives about 95% for 21-hydroxylase, and MSD says 11β-hydroxylase deficiency "is responsible for up to 8% of all cases of congenital adrenal hyperplasia" ([MSD Manual: 11β-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-11beta-hydroxylase-deficiency)). Either way, 21-hydroxylase is by far the most common and 11β-hydroxylase is second (your notes, p. 56).

## Your notes, enzyme by enzyme (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, pp. 56-57)
- **21-hydroxylase** turns 17-hydroxyprogesterone into 11-deoxycortisol (cortisol branch) and progesterone into 11-deoxycorticosterone (aldosterone branch). Blocked, both precursors are pushed into androgens, and high ACTH adds more. Girls have ambiguous genitals; boys look normal at birth. Severe cases waste salt: vomiting, low blood pressure, low sodium, high potassium and high renin; low cortisol can also cause **hypoglycemia** and circulatory collapse. A high 17-hydroxyprogesterone is diagnostic, often found on newborn screening. **Treatment:** low, physiologic doses of glucocorticoid to switch off the extra ACTH and so the extra androgens.
- **11β-hydroxylase** turns 11-deoxycorticosterone into corticosterone and 11-deoxycortisol into cortisol. Girls have ambiguous genitals. 11-deoxycorticosterone, a weak mineralocorticoid, builds up and causes **low-renin hypertension with low potassium** even though aldosterone itself is low.
- **17α-hydroxylase** works in both the adrenal and the gonads. Without it there are no androgens, estrogens or cortisol, but the mineralocorticoid pathway is overdriven by ACTH (more 11-deoxycorticosterone and corticosterone). 46,XY babies look female at birth but have no uterus or upper vagina; 46,XX babies are normal at birth. At puberty neither develops secondary sex traits and girls have no periods; hypertension, low potassium and low renin are usually found around then.

## High-yield facts
- Newborn screening in the US measures 17-OHP at 2-4 days of life; a positive screen is confirmed with a repeat 17-OHP and electrolytes. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/); MSD confirms newborn screening measures 17-OHP: [MSD Manual: 21-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-21-hydroxylase-deficiency) (the 2-4 day timing is standard teaching)
- Classic salt-wasting 21-hydroxylase deficiency presents in early infancy with low sodium and volume depletion (adrenal crisis). [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/), [MSD Manual: 21-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-21-hydroxylase-deficiency)
- Why 11β-hydroxylase deficiency is hypertensive even without aldosterone: DOC is itself a mineralocorticoid and accumulates behind the block. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/), [MSD Manual: 11β-Hydroxylase Deficiency](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/endocrine-disorders-in-children/congenital-adrenal-hyperplasia-caused-by-11beta-hydroxylase-deficiency)
- Rarer forms: 3β-hydroxysteroid dehydrogenase (salt wasting, ambiguous genitals in both sexes) and StAR deficiency (lipoid CAH: all adrenal steroids low, salt wasting, female-appearing 46,XY). [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/) (standard teaching; not on the openable MSD pages)

## Clinical vignette
A 2-week-old infant with ambiguous genitalia (enlarged clitoris, fused labia) and a 46,XX karyotype is brought in with vomiting and poor feeding. Sodium 126 mEq/L, potassium 6.8 mEq/L. Which serum precursor is most elevated?

**Answer:** 17-hydroxyprogesterone (classic salt-wasting 21-hydroxylase deficiency).
**Why:** the block stops both cortisol and aldosterone production (low sodium, high potassium). Precursors build up before the block and are pushed into androgens, which virilize a 46,XX infant. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/)
**Trap:** choosing 11-deoxycortisol (11β-hydroxylase deficiency), which causes hypertension and low potassium, not salt wasting.

## Common traps
- Salt wasting plus virilization = 21-hydroxylase. Hypertension plus virilization = 11β-hydroxylase. Hypertension plus no virilization = 17α-hydroxylase.
- In 17α-hydroxylase deficiency, a 46,XX girl has normal-looking genitals at birth and presents later with absent puberty and hypertension.
- A 46,XY child with 17α-hydroxylase deficiency looks female but has **no internal female organs** (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, p. 57).
- A salt-wasting infant may also be **hypoglycemic** from low cortisol (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, p. 56).
- Glucocorticoid treatment works by lowering ACTH, not by blocking androgens directly (El-Husseiny Endocrinology, p. 56).
- Renin follows the mineralocorticoid effect: high when salt is wasted, suppressed when DOC is in excess.

## Practice questions
1. A 15-year-old with a female appearance, no breast development, hypertension and potassium 3.0 mEq/L has a 46,XY karyotype. Which enzyme is deficient?
   - **Answer:** 17α-hydroxylase.
   - **Why:** no sex steroids (no puberty, undervirilized 46,XY) and excess DOC (hypertension, low potassium). [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/)
   - **Trap:** 11β-hydroxylase also causes hypertension but with androgen excess.
2. A 4-year-old boy has pubic hair, rapid growth and blood pressure 130/88 mmHg. Renin is suppressed. Which precursor is elevated?
   - **Answer:** 11-deoxycortisol (and DOC), 11β-hydroxylase deficiency.
   - **Why:** androgen excess causes early pubarche; DOC excess causes hypertension and suppressed renin. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/)
   - **Trap:** 21-hydroxylase also causes early pubarche, but blood pressure is low or normal.
3. Why are the adrenal glands enlarged in all forms of CAH?
   - **Answer:** chronically high ACTH.
   - **Why:** low cortisol removes negative feedback; ACTH drives adrenal growth. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/)
   - **Trap:** thinking the excess androgens cause the enlargement.
4. What is the inheritance pattern of CAH?
   - **Answer:** autosomal recessive.
   - **Why:** both copies of the enzyme gene must be defective. [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK448098/)
   - **Trap:** MEN syndromes are autosomal dominant; do not mix them up.
